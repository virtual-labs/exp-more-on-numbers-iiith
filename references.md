1. **David M. Burton, Elementary Number Theory (7th Edition).**
   - Comprehensive introduction to number theory, including divisibility and digital roots.

2. **Paul Zeitz, The Art and Craft of Problem Solving.**
   - Problem-solving strategies and number theory puzzles, including digit-based tricks.

3. **Wikipedia: Divisibility rule**  
   https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divisibility_rule
   - Theory and examples of divisibility tests.

4. **Wikipedia: Digital root**  
   https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_root
   - Explanation and properties of digital roots.

5. **NPTEL: Introduction to Number Theory**  
   https://nptel.ac.in/courses/111/106/111106139/
   - Video lectures and notes on number theory topics.
